THE PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Research that begins where comfortable narratives end.

Dr. S. Yeshwant Raj is an Assistant Professor in the International Business Administration Program at Tunghai University. His research examines entrepreneurship, innovation, artificial intelligence, organizational behavior, sustainability, and technology-enabled learning.

His work asks difficult but necessary questions: When does AI support become dependency? Why does innovation energize some people while unsettling others? Can entrepreneurship education build judgment rather than merely intention? What hidden costs sit behind digital convenience?

RESEARCH DOMAINS

Not topics. Tensions.

We organize research around contradictions that conventional frameworks often flatten.

01

AI augmentation vs. dependency

How intelligent systems enhance performance while reshaping independent thinking, learning, trust, and decision quality.

02

Entrepreneurial intention vs. capability

How practice-based learning, self-efficacy, alertness, and experimentation convert aspiration into entrepreneurial judgment.

03

Innovation opportunity vs. human cost

How emerging technologies affect creativity, job insecurity, burnout, motivation, and organizational adaptation.

04

Digital convenience vs. sustainability

How frictionless platforms, digital payments, logistics, and rebound effects produce invisible environmental consequences.

05

Structure vs. entrepreneurial freedom

How organizational systems create coordination while potentially constraining improvisation, bricolage, and venture behavior.

06

Answers vs. learning loops

How Socratic AI loops and feedback architectures can prevent cognitive bypass and strengthen durable learning.

RESEARCH METHODS / FROM QUESTION TO EVIDENCE

Question the model. Then test it.

Our methodological approach begins with a clear research problem, careful measurement, and an explicit account of what the data can support.

01 / EXPLAIN

Quantitative & econometric research

Hierarchical regression, robust standard errors, mediation, moderation, panel and longitudinal modeling.

02 / PREDICT

Machine learning & validation

Regularized models, gradient boosting, out-of-sample validation and model performance across contexts.

03 / VERIFY

Secondary data & documentary research

Cross-national indicators, corporate reporting, documentary coding, traceable sources and dataset construction.

04 / EXPERIMENT

Behavioral & educational research

Multi-wave studies, experiential learning, intervention design and human–AI decision processes.

RESEARCH INTEGRITY

Construct validity. Transparent measurement. Alternative explanations. Robustness. Responsible AI use. Ethical handling of research data.
